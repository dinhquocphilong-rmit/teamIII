The '67' Meme: Christian Bale Rant

What is the '67' Meme?

The '67' meme refers to the infamous "Christian Bale Rant," a viral audio clip from the set of the 2009 film Terminator Salvation. It's cataloged as entry #67 in the Know Your Meme encyclopedia. The meme captures actor Christian Bale's explosive outburst against director of photography Shane Hurlbut, featuring profanity-laced tirades about frustration and rage. It has become a classic internet meme used to depict over-the-top anger, celebrity meltdowns, and humorous outbursts.

Origin

The meme originated from an unedited audio recording leaked on February 2, 2009, by TMZ. On the set of Terminator Salvation in July 2008, Bale berated Hurlbut for interrupting a scene by walking into the shot. The four-minute rant includes phrases like "Don't you know who the fuck I am?" and "I will fucking kill you!" Bale's intense method acting style and reputation as a serious actor made the clip instantly viral, transforming him from a "fairy-tale hero" into a meme icon.

Spread and Cultural Impact

The clip spread rapidly across blogs, Twitter, and media outlets. It inspired remixes, soundboards, and parodies, including TV shows like Family Guy. At the 2011 Oscars, Bale referenced it humorously in his acceptance speech. The meme's raw emotion makes it relatable for venting frustration and has influenced modern audio memes on platforms like TikTok.

Examples

Popular adaptations include soundboards on sites like UGO, YouTube remixes (e.g., "Bale Out"), and visual memes with captions like "WHY DON'T YOU F------ UNDERSTAND?" The original audio can be found on various archives. Modern takes include AI-generated videos and reaction compilations.
